Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026

This policy explains how personal data is handled when you visit trenchwatch.dev or write to us. In short: the only personal data we keep is what you send us to get in touch, and we never sell, rent or give it to anyone.

1. Who is responsible

Trenchwatch is a product of EMERGE, operated by Ivan Alonso Herrezuelo, Barcelona, Spain, who is the data controller. Contact for anything related to your data: info@trenchwatch.dev.

2. What we collect

This website has no sign-up, no account, no contact form and no newsletter. We only receive personal data when you choose to contact us, by email or another channel you pick. That can include your name, email address, organisation, role and whatever you write in your message.

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we do not build profiles of visitors.

3. Why we use it and on what legal basis

  • To answer your message and, if you ask for it, to send information about our products, test access or a proposal. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before a possible contract, and our legitimate interest in replying to people who contact us (Article 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).
  • To keep a record of the conversation with you or your organisation while it is useful for that relationship. Legal basis: legitimate interest.

We never use your data for anything else, we do not send you marketing you did not ask for, and we do not make automated decisions about you.

4. We do not sell or share your data

We do not sell, rent, exchange or give your personal data to anyone for their own purposes. The only parties that touch it are the service providers that make our email and website work, acting on our behalf and under their data-processing terms:

  • Cloudflare: hosts the website and receives email sent to info@trenchwatch.dev. Like any web host, it processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. We do not use that data to identify visitors.
  • Google (Gmail): the mailbox where your emails are stored.
  • Brevo: the service through which our replies are sent.
  • Google Fonts: the site loads its typefaces from Google’s servers, so your browser sends your IP address to Google when it fetches them. Google states that this service does not set cookies or use that data to profile you.

We will only disclose data to an authority if a law or a court order requires it.

5. International transfers

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area, mainly in the United States. Where they do, the transfer is covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or by the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your messages only as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and the relationship that follows. If there is no ongoing relationship, we delete them within 24 months of the last exchange, or sooner if you ask. We will keep data longer only where a law requires it.

7. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to receive it in a portable format. Just write to info@trenchwatch.dev. We will reply within one month. You also have the right to complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or the authority in your own country.

8. Security

We use providers with recognised security standards, encrypted connections (HTTPS and TLS for email) and restricted access to our mailboxes.

9. On-chain data

Trenchwatch analyses public blockchain data (Solana). That analysis describes addresses and on-chain patterns, never individuals, and is not part of what this policy covers. If you believe an address we refer to relates to you, write to us and we will review it.

10. Children

Our services are meant for businesses and professionals and are not aimed at anyone under 18.

11. Changes

If we change how we handle data, we will update this page and the date at the top. See also our Cookie Policy.
