Cookie Policy

Effective 23 November 2026

In short: trenchwatch.dev does not use cookies, neither its own nor third-party ones, for analytics, advertising or tracking. That is why you will not see a cookie banner: there is nothing to ask your consent for.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website can store in your browser to remember information about you or your visit. Similar technologies (such as local storage or tracking pixels) work in a comparable way; this policy covers them too.

2. What this site uses

trenchwatch.dev is an informational website. It has no login, no saved preferences, no analytics and no advertising, so it does not set any cookies or similar technologies in your browser.

3. Third-party resources

  • Google Fonts: the site loads its typefaces from Google’s servers. According to Google, this service does not set cookies. Your browser does send your IP address to Google to fetch the font files, as with any resource loaded from another server.
  • Cloudflare: our web host. It may use strictly necessary technical measures to protect the site against attacks and abuse. These do not track you and do not require consent.
  • External links: if you follow a link to another site (for example X or GitHub), that site’s own cookie policy applies.

4. How to control cookies

You can block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings. Because this site does not set any, doing so will not affect how it works.

5. If this changes

If we ever add a tool that uses non-essential cookies, we will update this page first and ask for your consent before it loads, as the law requires.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy: info@trenchwatch.dev. See also our Privacy Policy.
